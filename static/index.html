About Us

Questian Academy ek online learning platform hai jahan aap modern technology skills seekh sakte hain. Yahan aap Web Development, Cyber Security, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, aur programming languages ki courses kar sakte hain.

Hamara maqsad hai ke Pakistan aur duniya bhar ke students ko affordable aur quality education mile. Har course mein video lectures, notes, aur MCQ tests shamil hain. Hamara AI assistant 24/7 aapki madad ke liye available hai.

What We Offer

  • 📚 12+ Professional Courses — Web, AI, Cyber Security, Programming
  • 🎥 Video Lectures — Har course ke saath YouTube videos
  • 📝 MCQ Tests — Apna knowledge check karein
  • 📥 Notes Download — Study material PDF format mein
  • 🤖 AI Assistant — 24/7 sawalon ke jawab
  • 💬 Teacher Chat — Direct teachers se baat karein
  • 📊 Progress Tracking — Apni learning track karein
